Tiwa Savage

A-Baddie

A film by Troy Roscoe

The idea

Iconic frames, in the vastness of the desert.

We take Tiwa and her women out into open sand and build a run of images that feel lifted from the pages of a fashion magazine. Each setup is composed like a still, then brought to life by choreography.

These are the frames people save, screenshot and pin, the ones other directors will use as their own references for years.

The world is bleached and muted. The only colour on screen is Tiwa's yellow.

The Colourama

A yellow colourama in the middle of nowhere, crew and all. Tiwa poses through a run of bold looks. Then she turns, rips a hole straight through the backdrop and walks through it. We follow, and cut seamlessly into the mirror field.

The Mirror Field

She walks out of the colourama into a field of mirrors driven into the sand, each at its own angle and depth. Before she dances, she grabs her outfit, rips it away and reveals a new one underneath.

Erratic handheld mixed with still, composed frames. Every mirror is buried at its own angle. Some women dance to their reflection, others to camera.

Upside Down

Tiwa on a lifeguard chair, facing a giant mirror that holds the world the wrong way up. In the edit the whole frame flips, so the sand becomes the sky.

08

The Loom

Overhead. Long strips of aso-oke run across the sand as the warp, and the women become the weft, woven into the cloth itself. Some lie on their backs, some on their fronts. Strips pass over one body and under the next, limbs showing in some places and wrapped in others.

Tiwa lies along the centre, the thread that holds it together.

The references are a start. On the day it's a true basket weave of bodies and cloth.

Palette & grade

Bleached, with one colour left alive.

The palette follows the brief: muted nudes, sand, taupe, cream and a faded oxblood. The grade is a bleached film print, with lifted milky blacks, soft halation, sun-washed highlights and real grain. Yellow is keyed and isolated, the only thing on screen at full strength. Wherever Tiwa is, the eye goes.
