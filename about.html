About

Troy Roscoe

Troy Roscoe is a UK-based director working across music video and branded content, with credits for artists including GloRilla, Burna Boy, KSI, and Ed Sheeran.

He came to directing without a traditional route in. There was no film school — the craft was learned by pulling apart tutorials, saving for basic camera kit, and figuring it out through the work itself. What started as something built on the side eventually became a directing career on its own terms.

That path — self-taught, non-linear, built through persistence rather than connections — now also shapes his teaching and mentoring work through the Creative Goldmine book series, which has reached an audience of 250,000+ people navigating their own creative careers.

Today his work spans high-concept music video treatments and branded campaigns, with an emphasis on strong visual worlds built around a clear, ownable idea.
