About

Troy Roscoe

Troy Roscoe is a UK-based director working across music video and branded content.

After finishing a degree in marketing, he picked up a camera looking for something to be passionate about — and found it. Early on, he built a name doing everything himself: shooting, editing and executing his own VFX to create content that went viral online, long before he ever stepped onto a professional set.

That hands-on background in editing, CGI and post-production still shapes how he directs today. Knowing exactly what's achievable in the edit means bigger, more ambitious ideas can be brought to life with total clarity on set — a skill that consistently helps productions move faster and aim higher.
